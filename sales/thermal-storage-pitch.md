## The problem every facility manager knows

- **The grid:** the 3 AM outage nobody can explain, and a 3-year wait for the utility to approve a new interconnection.

## The solution

A solar thermal power plant that stores heat, not electrons, and delivers power around the clock:

1. Parabolic troughs focus sunlight on a pipe and heat the liquid inside.
2. The hot liquid is pumped into insulated storage tanks.
3. The tanks hold that heat 24/7.
4. A heat exchanger turns it into low-pressure vapor.
5. The vapor spins a turbine and generates electricity.
6. Power is dispatched on demand to your facility.

**Need cooling too?** The same heat loop drives an absorption chiller for industrial chilled water or process heat. Minimal moving parts, no synthetic refrigerant gases.
